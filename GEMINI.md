# Quirk Project Rules

## Communication and Writing Invariants

1. **Strict unslop standard**:
   - Zero AI conversational boilerplate (no "Certainly!", "I hope this helps", "Great question!").
   - Cut puffery, buzzwords, and vague marketing filler.
   - Do not use em dashes (—). Use periods, commas, or clean sentence breaks.
   - Write all headings in sentence case.
   - Do not use decorative emojis in headings or bullet points.
   - Use active voice, simple language, and direct facts.

2. **Brand identity**:
   - Primary: `#ABFF2A` (Lime), Secondary: `#00D4FF` (Cyan), Background: `#080B10`, Surface: `#11161D`, Borders: `#22303A`.
   - Typography: Satoshi for headings, Inter for body copy, JetBrains Mono for metrics and code.

3. **Development standards**:
   - Monorepo package manager is `pnpm`.
   - Front-end is located in `apps/dashboard` (Vite, React, `@tanstack/react-router`, Tailwind).
